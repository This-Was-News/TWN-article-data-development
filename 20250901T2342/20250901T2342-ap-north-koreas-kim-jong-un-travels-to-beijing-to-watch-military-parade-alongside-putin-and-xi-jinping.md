## Article summary
North Korean leader Kim Jong Un is traveling to Beijing by train to attend a military parade with Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese President Xi Jinping. The event commemorates the 80th anniversary of the end of World War II and China's fight against Japanese aggression. This marks Kim's first attendance at a major multilateral event during his 14-year rule and the first time for Kim, Xi, and Putin, all key challengers of the U.S., to gather at the same venue. Kim left Pyongyang for Beijing on Monday, traveling with top officials, including Foreign Minister Choe Son Hu. His train is expected to arrive in Beijing on Tuesday after stopping at the Chinese border city of Dandong. This is Kim's first visit to China since 2019 and his fifth visit since inheriting power in late 2011. Putin arrived in China on Sunday to attend the Shanghai Cooperation Organization summit and the Beijing parade. Observers are keenly watching for potential bilateral or trilateral meetings among the three leaders. North Korea's foreign policy priority has been Russia in recent years, as it supports Russia's war against Ukraine in exchange for economic and military assistance. Kim's presence at the multilateral event in Beijing is seen as part of efforts to develop partnerships with nations close to China and Russia. Meanwhile, North Korea has been shunning talks with the U.S. and South Korea, focusing instead on expanding its nuclear and missile arsenals.
