## Article summary
The article discusses the deployment of National Guard troops from Republican-led states to Washington, D.C., to support President Trump's crackdown on crime, while these states themselves grapple with high crime rates. Tennessee's governor, Bill Lee, sent troops to Washington despite Memphis and Nashville having higher crime rates than the nation's capital. Other Republican governors, such as Greg Abbott in Texas, Mike DeWine in Ohio, and Mike Kehoe in Missouri, face similar situations but have not sought federal intervention. The article highlights a list of cities in Republican-led states with crime rates comparable to Washington's. Critics question why these governors do not address crime in their own states instead of deploying troops elsewhere. The piece also notes the political tensions and divisions exacerbated by these deployments, with Democrats expressing concerns about the implications of such actions. The article concludes by emphasizing the disparity between the rhetoric and the reality of crime and federal intervention in these states.
