## Article summary
President Trump's executive orders have stripped nearly half a million federal workers of their union protections. In August, 22 agencies moved to ignore collective bargaining contracts for nearly one million workers, following an appeals court ruling. Trump justified this by stating that the affected workers' roles touch on national security and that their labor contracts could interfere with his policies. The orders targeted federal labor unions, which have repeatedly sued the Trump administration. Agencies like the Environmental Protection Agency, U.S. Coast Guard, and Department of Veterans Affairs have terminated union contracts covering over 445,000 workers. This action has been described as the largest act of union busting in American history.
