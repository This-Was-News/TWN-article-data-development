## Article summary
President Trump claims his administration's policies are designed to support American workers, promote high-paying blue-collar jobs, and rebuild the nation's industrial base. However, labor leaders strongly disagree, with AFL-CIO President Liz Shuler stating that this administration has been the most hostile to workers in their lifetimes. While economic indicators like wage growth and low unemployment suggest workers are in decent shape, there is significant uncertainty and tension. Trump's immigration policies have disrupted various industries, leading to job losses and increased workloads for remaining workers. Unions, including those in Trump's base, are concerned about the future of infrastructure projects and the administration's actions, such as the stop-work order on the Revolution Wind farm, which has left hundreds of workers idle. Additionally, the federal workforce is experiencing a mass exodus, with about 300,000 employees expected to leave by year's end, raising concerns about the government's ability to deliver essential services. The impact of these changes will become clearer over the next three years.
