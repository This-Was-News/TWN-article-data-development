## Article summary
In fiscal year 2025, Texas reported 259,878 illegal border crossers, excluding gotaways. This figure represents approximately 10% of the total illegal entries in Texas for 2022 and 2023. Gotaway data, obtained by The Center Square, indicates that at least two million gotaways have entered the U.S. during the Biden administration. Many of these gotaways are known to have criminal records, including individuals on the terrorist watchlist and Special Interest Aliens (SIAs) who pose a national security risk. In fiscal year 2023 alone, Texas reported nearly 450,000 known gotaways, accounting for nearly 60% of the total reported across the southwest border. The El Paso and Del Rio sectors saw the highest numbers, with gotaways comprising almost half of Border Patrol apprehensions in 2023. Texas's Operation Lone Star continues to apprehend gotaways, alleged smugglers, and other criminals, with approximately 100 criminal arrests made weekly. This operation has also identified thousands of foreign nationals with active felony warrants, including SIAs from various countries. Despite a decrease in illegal entries this year, the flow of gotaways into Texas persists, highlighting ongoing border security challenges.
