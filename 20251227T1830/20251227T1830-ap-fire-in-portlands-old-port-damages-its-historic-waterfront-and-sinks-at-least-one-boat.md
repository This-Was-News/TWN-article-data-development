## Article summary
A fire significantly damaged Portland, Maine's historic Old Port waterfront the day after Christmas. The blaze spread rapidly through aging structures along Custom House Wharf, a key area for the city's fishing industry that also houses seafood restaurants. Firefighters faced challenges due to frozen hydrants, utilizing a fire boat to combat the flames. Several boats were impacted, with at least one sinking. The cause of the fire remains under investigation. One firefighter sustained minor injuries. The Porthole Restaurant, located on the wharf, confirmed that all staff, fishermen, and owners were safe.
