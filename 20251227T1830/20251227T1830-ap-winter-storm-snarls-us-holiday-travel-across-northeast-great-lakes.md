## Article summary
A significant winter storm caused widespread travel disruptions across the Northeast and Great Lakes regions of the United States during the busy holiday period between Christmas and New Year's. Over 1,500 flights were canceled starting Friday night, impacting thousands of travelers at major airports like Newark Liberty, JFK, and LaGuardia, which had issued snow warnings. While New York City received about four inches of snow, some areas on Long Island and in the Catskills experienced over six to ten inches, respectively. The National Weather Service had warned of hazardous travel conditions, including potential tree damage and power outages. Governors of New York and New Jersey declared states of emergency in anticipation of the severe weather. Despite the disruptions, by Saturday morning, the storm began to weaken, and roads and skies started to clear. Workers were seen clearing snow in Times Square, with some tourists finding the snowfall beautiful and a "Christmas surprise." The storm's rapid movement from northwest to southeast was noted by forecasters. The weather conditions prompted advisories for travelers to monitor conditions and plan accordingly.
