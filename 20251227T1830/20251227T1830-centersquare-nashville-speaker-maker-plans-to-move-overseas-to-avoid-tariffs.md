## Article summary
A Nashville-based speaker company, Auratone, known for its legendary 5C Super Sound Cube used by artists like Michael Jackson and Taylor Swift, is planning to move its production overseas. This decision comes as a direct response to the unpredictable and increasing tariffs imposed by the Trump administration. Alex Jacobsen, the owner and president, stated that over 60% of his clients are international, making tariffs a significant obstacle. The company, founded in 1958, has faced difficulties in planning and fiscal management due to the fluctuating tariff landscape. Jacobsen cited a $4,000 bill incurred due to tariffs on imported parts as an example of the financial strain. The uncertainty surrounding tariff policies, with frequent changes, has paralyzed the business. Auratone's annual sales are approximately $400,000, and the company currently has two part-time employees. Jacobsen hopes a Supreme Court ruling on the legality of these tariffs will bring much-needed stability. The company will begin manufacturing some loudspeakers abroad for the first time in its 66-year history starting in 2026.
