## Article summary
The president of the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts, Richard Grenell, has threatened legal action against musician Chuck Redd for canceling an annual Christmas Eve jazz concert. Redd, who had hosted the concert for nearly two decades, cited his protest against the institution's new name, the Trump-Kennedy Center, as the reason for his withdrawal. Grenell, appointed by President Trump, characterized Redd's decision as "sad bullying tactics" and "classic intolerance," asserting that it would cost the non-profit arts institution $1 million in damages. The Kennedy Center's board of trustees, largely handpicked by President Trump, voted to change the name, a move that broke with the center's bipartisan governance. President Trump himself expressed surprise and honor at the renaming, which occurred shortly before Christmas. The renaming has also drawn a lawsuit from Representative Joyce Beatty, who argues that an act of Congress is necessary to alter the center's name, which was originally designated as a memorial to President John F. Kennedy by Congress in 1964.
