## Article summary
President Donald Trump has stated that the upcoming 2026 midterm elections will center on the issue of pricing, signaling a shift in his economic messaging. He argues that his administration brought down high prices, particularly for energy and gasoline, contrasting this with the Biden administration's record. Trump highlighted recent inflation reports showing cooling price increases and noted that conservative states experienced lower inflation rates than liberal states, especially in energy costs. He also pointed to a recent government report indicating a strong annualized economic growth rate of 4.3% in the third quarter. Following the November elections where Democrats saw overperformance among voters concerned with affordability, Republicans are refining their economic messaging. Trump has launched a speaking tour to discuss his role in lowering grocery and gas prices through policies like tariffs, while also attributing current inflation rates to Democrats. He previously dismissed affordability concerns as a Democratic "con job" and "hoax." Trump also referred to the Epstein files as a distraction from his administration's achievements. The next economic challenge he faces is a potential government shutdown at the end of January, linked to the debate over extending the Affordable Care Act's premium tax credits.
