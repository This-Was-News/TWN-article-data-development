## Article summary
Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has stated that Russia is allegedly planning to interfere with a potential Ukrainian referendum on President Donald Trump's 20-point peace plan. Zelensky's assertion, based on intelligence from Ukraine's Foreign Intelligence Service, suggests Russia intends to allow Ukrainian citizens in Russia and occupied territories to vote, aiming to undermine future Ukrainian elections. This alleged Russian interference aims to delegitimize Ukrainian authorities. The prospect of a referendum is contingent on Russia agreeing to a 60-day ceasefire, a key point Zelensky plans to discuss with Trump. Ukrainian negotiators consider territorial concessions to Russia a red line, while pushing for strong security guarantees from the United States. These demands have been significant obstacles in peace talks. Meanwhile, Russia launched a large-scale attack on Kyiv with drones and missiles, targeting energy facilities and civilian infrastructure, resulting in casualties. Zelensky highlighted this attack as Russia's response to the peace negotiations and emphasized the need for robust air defense systems and security guarantees for Ukraine, especially if a referendum proceeds. He stressed the importance of safe skies and the presence of observers during any voting process.
